### Status of Activities

- **Attestation Specifications:** Following previous discussions on lightweight and heavyweight attestations, the group identified an opportunity to start drafting technical specifications. It was noted that this specification should look beyond just the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), as the European Commission is utilising the term "attestations" in broader contexts.  
- **Manufacturer Survey:** A survey is being developed to evaluate the appetite and conditions under which manufacturers will adopt and rely on open-source component attestations. Creating a sustainable ecosystem requires ensuring these attestations actually address manufacturer liability and compliance needs. Draft questions for the survey will be shared for review at the next meeting.

### Attestations and ENISA Playbook alignment \#40

- **Call for Feedback:** Feedback was requested on the open points of the ongoing playbook document. The primary goal of this document is to build a bridge for manufacturers, making open-source attestations more accessible and appealing to them. Participants agreed to coordinate offline to review the document in detail.

### Code & Compliance FOSDEM Workshop

- **Workshop Format:** A proposal was presented for an upcoming event, dedicating half of the agenda to hands-on workshops.  
- **Core Topics:** The workshop will centre on three intertwined concepts:  
  - Attestations and how they function.  
  - Due diligence processes for open-source components and their reliance on attestations.  
  - The sustainability of open source and the role of stewardship.  
- **Methodology:** To avoid unstructured discussions, participants will be split into diverse small groups and provided with pre-read materials and specific questions to answer. The outcomes will be summarised post-workshop to capture the community's consensus.  
- **Next Steps:** A formal outline will be sent to the mailing list for community feedback, and a call for volunteer group chairs will be issued shortly.

### AOB

- European policy updates:  
  - **European Commission Initiatives:** Recent engagements with the European Commission (including the Future Internet Unit) indicate active development of a "Stewardship Toolkit," which is expected to include Voluntary Security Attestations (VSAs), likely formalised into law via an implementing act.  
  - **European Digital Infrastructure Consortium (EDIC):** It was highlighted that EDIC is partnering with sovereign tech initiatives to Europeanise the identification and funding of critical open-source infrastructure. Current adoption is limited to a few member states (e.g., France, Germany, Netherlands, Italy). Participants were encouraged to raise awareness of EDIC within their respective national governments.  
  - **Training and OSPOs:** Upcoming initiatives for 2027 include an Erasmus program promoting OSS principles and skills training for government employees under the Interoperable Europe Academy. The underlying goal appears to be the establishment of an Open Source Program Office (OSPO) in every European country.  
  - **New OSS Assessment Framework:** An upcoming assessment framework meant to assist with due diligence for open source was discussed.  
    - *Concerns Raised:* Participants expressed concern that traditional organisational or governance assessments are ill-suited for standard open-source projects (which are often maintained by single individuals) and that most projects would fail such assessments by default.  
    - *Action Item:* It was emphasised that the community must proactively engage with these assessment frameworks to ensure they reflect the realities of open-source development.
